Vanilla Strawberry Cake

$42.00

Our Vanilla Strawberry Cake pairs delicate vanilla sponge with smooth cream and fresh strawberries. Each bite offers a light, creamy texture with a gentle sweetness and bright fruit flavor.

Finished with fresh strawberries, it is a timeless and elegant dessert with a touch of summer freshness.

PROCESS

Layered vanilla sponge with fresh strawberries and smooth cream.

INGREDIENTS

Wheat flour, eggs, sugar, butter, milk, cream, fresh strawberries, vanilla.

ALLERGENS

Contains Wheat, Eggs, and Milk. Made in a facility that also processes tree nuts.
