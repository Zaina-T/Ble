Pistachio & White Chocolate

$5.00

Our Pistachio & White Chocolate Cookie combines delicate roasted pistachios with creamy white chocolate in a rich, buttery dough. Each bite offers a refined balance of nutty depth, gentle sweetness, and a soft, tender texture.

Baked until lightly golden around the edges, it remains beautifully soft at the center. A sophisticated blend of flavors, crafted for those who enjoy a delicate twist on a classic cookie.

PROCESS

Handcrafted dough with roasted pistachios and white chocolate, baked fresh until golden.

INGREDIENTS

Organic wheat flour, butter, white chocolate, roasted pistachios, brown sugar, eggs, vanilla, sea salt.

ALLERGENS

Contains Wheat, Milk, Eggs, and Tree Nuts (Pistachios). Made in a facility that also processes other tree nuts.
