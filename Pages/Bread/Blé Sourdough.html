Blé Sourdough

$8.50

Our signature loaf, born from a meticulously maintained starter and a patient 48- hour fermentation process. This slow development allows the complex, tangy flavor profile to fully blossom while creating an impossibly airy, open crumb.

Crafted daily using a proprietary blend of stone-ground heritage flours sourced directly from local millers. The result is a deeply caramelized, blistered crust that provides a satisfying shatter, giving way to a tender, deeply flavorful interior.

PROCESS

48-hour natural fermentation

INGREDIENTS

Organic stone-ground wheat flour, filtered water, sea salt, wild yeast culture.

ALLERGENS

Contains Wheat. Made in a facility that also processes tree nuts.
