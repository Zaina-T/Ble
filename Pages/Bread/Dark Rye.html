Dark Rye

$9.00

Our Dark Rye is a hearty loaf crafted with rich rye flour and a naturally fermented starter. Its deep, earthy flavor and dense yet tender crumb create a satisfying bite with every slice.

Baked with a beautifully dark, rustic crust, it carries subtle notes of toasted grain and spice. Full-bodied and wholesome, it pairs wonderfully with savory toppings or simply a touch of butter.

PROCESS

Slow natural fermentation for a deep, developed flavor

INGREDIENTS

Dark rye flour, organic wheat flour, filtered water, sea salt, wild yeast culture.

ALLERGENS

Contains Wheat and Rye. Made in a facility that also processes tree nuts.
